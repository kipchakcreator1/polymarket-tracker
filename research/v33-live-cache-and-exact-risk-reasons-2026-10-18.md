## Durable reason-attributed sports results (new read-only release)

The risk-reason instrumentation in the working bot was initially absent from the separate long-running outcome checkpoint: that archive retained the generic decision but dropped the exact veto reason after v2 settlement-status refreshes. This has now been repaired **only in the audit/research collector** by `capture_original_risk_verdict()`: an original observed quote ID/time, risk-check time, final decision and gate must all agree. Subsequent classification refreshes retain immutable evidence even if the source 900-entry quote ring evicts the trade. Historical reason-less records remain explicitly **legacy/unknown**, and fake mismatched timestamps, gates or source-control characters fail closed.

A separate `tools/v33_risk_reason_outcome_audit.py` aggregates final and unresolved position observations per **proven** risk code, original book quote and unique market condition. It excludes condition collisions across the entire archive, quotes formed after settlement, missing original ask/depth and unknown outcomes from modeled HOLD ROI. No user orders, true fills, causal filter benefits or independent game-level labels can be inferred.
